# Getting help

- **A question** about running a node, joining a pool, lending a graphics card or using the desktop app: ask in [Discussions, Q&A](https://github.com/sisyphus-network/Sisyphus/discussions/categories/q-a). Someone who has run into it before may answer, and the answer stays for the next person.
- **Something broken**: open an [issue](https://github.com/sisyphus-network/Sisyphus/issues/new/choose) with what you did, what happened, and the node's output around it.
- **A vulnerability**: report it privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says, not in public.

Before asking, [Using Sisyphus](docs/user-guide.md) walks through the desktop app, and [`apps/sisyphusd/README.md`](apps/sisyphusd/README.md) through running nodes.

Sisyphus is an early prototype made by two people in the open. There is no paid support, and an answer may take a day or two.
