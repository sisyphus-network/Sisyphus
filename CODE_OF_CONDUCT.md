# Code of conduct

Sisyphus adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone speaks for the project.

In short: be considerate, assume good faith, criticise work and not people, and leave room for those who are new. Harassment, personal attacks and unwelcome sexual attention are not accepted in any form.

## Reporting

If someone's conduct concerns you, tell a maintainer privately: [@rvnminers-A-and-N](https://github.com/rvnminers-A-and-N) or [@excho0](https://github.com/excho0). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainers decide what happens, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines), from a private word up to a ban from the project.
